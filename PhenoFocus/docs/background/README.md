# 📚 Background notes

Study material Jiheon wrote while working out what PhenoFocus should be. These are **reading notes on other people's published work**, not PhenoFocus results — kept here because they are what the project's design rests on.

| File | Language | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| [`phenocompass_study_notes.md`](phenocompass_study_notes.md) | English | A self-contained explanation of the PhenoCompass preprint: what virtual screening is, why phenotypic virtual screening was hard, how geometric multimodal contrastive learning differs from direct CLIP-style alignment, how few-shot anchor scoring works, and the authors' own limitations. |
| [`phenocompass_review_outline.ko.md`](phenocompass_review_outline.ko.md) | Korean | Slide-by-slide outline for presenting that paper to the Functional Genomics team — each slide with an AI-side reading, a biology-side reading and a suggested visual. |
| [`lab_in_the_loop_talk_notes.ko.md`](lab_in_the_loop_talk_notes.ko.md) | Korean | Notes on a 32-slide industry talk on lab-in-the-loop drug R&D, covering target discovery, perturbation modelling, hit finding, phenotypic screening, antibody design and patient selection, each with follow-up literature. |

Two numbers from the PhenoCompass preprint that recur in the project README, both the authors' own results and not PhenoFocus results: a prospective screen of **3.8 billion** purchasable compounds, and **54×** enrichment of pathway-consistent hits over the training set, with 7 structurally novel inhibitors confirmed by orthogonal assays.
